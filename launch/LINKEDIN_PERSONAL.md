I've been building **7SVEN**, a local-first Android phone assistant under Ujexa.

The goal is to make on-device AI useful beyond answering questions: connecting a local language model to phone tools, memory, multi-step tasks, reminders, voice interaction and safety checks.

The current v1.4.1 development build has been exercised on a physical phone with core commands such as flashlight control, volume adjustment, opening Settings without changing anything, handling unsupported targets, and stopping/repeating commands.

It's still an early-stage project—not a Jarvis-level assistant, and not every Android app or task can be controlled reliably. I'm preparing the source repository and documenting the limitations, build requirements and third-party licenses before publishing.
